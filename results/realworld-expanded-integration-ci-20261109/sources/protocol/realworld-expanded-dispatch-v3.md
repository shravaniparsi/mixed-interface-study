# RealWorld exposed representation controls, dispatch v3

This extension admits exactly the seven records in the previously exercised realworld-paired-controls-v1 registry. Its canonical JSON digest fixes payloads, expectations, ordering and membership. The four baseline pairs remain route-only reference controls. The three representation variations are explicit payload interventions: duplicate/unordered article tags, null biography and omitted biography. They were already exercised in the prospective-control cohort; no untouched boundary is enabled by this entry point.

The duplicate-tag variation uses article-valid as its reference. Both biography variations use profile-bio-valid. References are selected before observations from the same workflow and producer. A reference repeat carries its own baseline pair identity and the triggering variation's target identity. The candidate's consumer order and the reference's own predeclared consumer order remain fixed. Exact job validation rejects substituting the variation for its reference or borrowing a control from another workflow.

The common v2 repeat policy is unchanged: a flagged observation receives two further candidate attempts and two baseline-reference attempts, with the original candidate and baseline observations retained. Stable complete observations require three complete candidate attempts and three clean reference attempts. A changing candidate stays variable; a failing reference cannot support stability. A cleanup failure halts later dispatch. These negative and repeat paths are qualified with synthetic expectation counterexamples based on archived raw observations, not claimed live application defects.

The source-pinned v2 pair classifier, prospective adapter, normalization, evidence capture, timing and process-scope helper are reused without modification. Versioned entry points extend only selection, job-to-reference binding and the source-qualified loader's runner identity. Existing baseline archives remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Ordinary live qualification expects seven clean pairs/fourteen branches with no follow-ups. Six selected entries belong to the retained inventory; the duplicate-tag engineering control is additional and must not inflate corpus coverage or independent sample size.

Each job retains its fresh fixture and two independent resets, exact scheduled identity/order, source copies, raw observations and scoped cleanup records. The 180-second job deadline, five-second termination grace and two-second kill confirmation are unchanged; the outer engineering suite is bounded to 480 seconds. An interrupted or incomplete runner halts the attempt and preserves evidence rather than being replaced. This stage adds no broader lifecycle fault-injection qualification.

The independent validator reconstructs pair observations, terminal envelopes and the full common-driver ledger. The complete evidence archive is separately hash-pinned and checked in CI. P2 remains open for legacy/generated RealWorld paths and PetClinic integration. Bounded primary batches, timing scope and final freeze remain outstanding. This is engineering qualification, not primary evaluation or efficacy evidence.
