## Claim architecture available from the new evidence

Question: does native continuation add a qualifying signal beyond a competent API-semantic comparator for independently specified inputs?

Supported finding: the closed independent-input cohort and benchmark extension supplied no qualifying incremental native-only signal under their respective qualification rules. This is conditional on the cases actually compared, with retained attrition and correlated repetitions. It is not evidence of system-wide equivalence or a prevalence estimate.

Supporting observations: the independent-input cohort executed 28 of 44 selected initial inputs; the benchmark extension retained all six selected families but scientifically executed only two GitLab workflows; the README run exposed a readback limitation that prevents treating every immediate state assertion as a valid durable-state verdict. These observations support a bounded feasibility report. They do not yet establish the original selector-method contribution or a separately validated measurement method.

This diagnosis does not rewrite the manuscript into a different research question.
