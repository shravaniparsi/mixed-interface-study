# Research feasibility decision after authored qualification

The current independent-input registration has no qualified real-system cases.
Its empirical contribution is not established, and it is not ready for a Q1
submission. The two successful authored fixtures qualify limited instrumentation;
they do not repair the missing empirical evidence. Stop expanding those fixtures
unless a concrete measurement failure requires it.

## Requirement audit

| Requirement | Current evidence | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure and sampling audit | Fixed catalog, retained exclusions and source ledgers | Missing historical artifacts and unresolved ancestry prevent a blanket independence claim. |
| Competent frozen baselines | Source-bound methods, corrected typed equality, passing authored controls | No qualified natural-system comparison; no superiority over source-aware replay established. |
| Prospective workflow selection | Twelve retained projects, with no replacement | Zero qualified scientific cases; bounded mapping unavailability is not exhaustive project ineligibility. |
| Bounded experiments | Eight terminal qualification failures and two successful authored runs | Scientific producer/continuation cases did not execute in this cohort. |
| Causal evidence | Known authored repair changes blocking to continuation | No natural-system causal attribution. |
| Costs within the ceiling | Scoped timing and current repository storage verified | A whole-project account invoice and historical spend remain unavailable. |
| Low manual study burden | Automated runners and observation verifiers | Real-workflow acquisition and state qualification have not demonstrated low manual effort. |
| Publication contribution | Adjacent literature checked and overclaims rejected | Neither sufficient empirical findings nor a demonstrated method advantage. |

## Budget evidence

Live metadata confirms the repository is public. Six artifact-metadata pages
returned a stable total of 580 unique artifacts, all unexpired, totaling
265,542,069 bytes (about 253 MiB). No contents were downloaded and no artifacts
were deleted. This is current repository storage, not accumulated billed storage,
deleted historical artifacts, account-wide Packages, caches or an invoice.

[GitHub's billing documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-actions)
states that standard public-repository runners are free, while larger runners
are billed. Storage and accrued usage are separate accounting considerations.
The eight catalog qualification jobs and two authored development jobs total
2,151 reported run seconds; each reports zero Ubuntu billable milliseconds.
Preparation/unit jobs and earlier studies are outside that subtotal. Do not
claim the entire project cost zero dollars or that the full $100 remains unused.

Compute is not the demonstrated primary obstacle. Additional free runner time
does not resolve sampling, source-aware baseline validity, real state restoration
or the absence of a scientific contribution.

## Concrete redesign for author consideration

The possible replacement question is: under what conditions do API-created
records remain editable through the native UI, and which prespecified repairs
restore editability without unintended changes? That is an empirical study of
compatibility and recovery. It differs from the closed study's exact native-JSON
request comparison and incremental-information estimand. It must not be presented
as the same experiment with easier gates.

If this direction is selected, the next deliverable is a prospective design,
not a new collection of application runs. It must specify:

1. A compatibility obligation supported by the application contract. An API-only
   administrative record is not automatically a defect because a UI cannot edit it.
2. Outcomes that separate rejection, normalization, blocked editing, successful
   editing, unintended writes, legitimate interface differences and missing data.
3. H/response, original-schema and competent source-aware baselines with explicit
   information access. Do not equate API success with correctness or claim an
   algorithmic advance from ordinary UI testing.
4. Original native controls and an application-specific complete state/actor
   procedure, qualified before altered inputs. The authored fixture procedure
   cannot simply be assumed to transfer to a database-backed application.
5. A prespecified repair/sham comparison with every permitted state difference
   enumerated before execution. No observed failure selects its own repair.
6. Outcome-independent population selection, exposure/lineage handling and fixed
   input ordering. Closed projects are not silently reopened or relabelled as
   independent. A new cohort cannot be selected merely to search for positives.
7. An explicit acquisition-effort budget, runtime/cost bounds, stopping rule and
   useful null-result criterion. Changing the question alone is not sufficient.

The alternative is to end this research direction and reassess topics against the
original budget and effort constraints. This is a change of research direction,
not a routine implementation choice. The author's preference has been requested;
no replacement question is treated as approved and no new empirical frame is
authorized by this document. The larger goal remains incomplete.
