The RealWorld adapters now enforce submitted method, target and payload equality; bind loaded articles and comments to returned producer resources; check settings fields against the bound user; and compare readable API-visible state before and after rejected requests. The reducer independently recomputes comparisons and checks their supporting trace records. A failed guard invalidates comparability rather than establishing an application defect.
