# Descriptive accounting for the frozen diagnostic collection

The primary report uses the existing source-qualified component maps. Its assigned-continuation overlap categories are API-only mismatch, native-UI-only mismatch, both, neither, rejected prefix, not comparison-eligible, unresolved, unavailable and native-only singleton. These are check signals, not confirmed independent defects. Shared final reads and shared persistence checks remain separately visible and receive no channel-exclusive credit. Missing or unknown evidence cannot become a clean comparison.

Only independently reconstructed, sealed attempt observations supply the analysis. A whole-unit acceptance failure prevents channel-comparison credit and cannot authorize a follow-up, even if a retained individual attempt appears clean. The preserved raw evidence, original terminal record and failure status remain available. No missing unit or failed attempt is replaced by a later success.

Repetitions use the frozen signatures and registered same-instrumentation references. Three stable candidate observations with clean references are labelled stable observations, not adjudicated defects. Incomplete repeats, failed controls, unknown semantics and variability stay explicit. Catalog overlap, development exposure and native-only controls remain in the report.

Timing measurements retain the pair, workflow, runtime profile/context, host image/platform, channel and candidate/control role. Descriptive summaries use those strata; they do not pool different candidates or workflows into a channel speed ranking. Controller setup and native pair fixture/frontend setup remain separate from branch intervals. The report does not supply an equal-budget selector comparison or population significance test.
