# Original research questions: principal evidence inventory

This pass inspected the principal summaries for the earlier diagnostic, recovery, production-extension, manual-contract, mutant and converter studies, alongside the closed independent-input and benchmark cohorts. It indexes their supported interpretations rather than revalidating every archived raw trace. The source files and selected values are hash-bound in manifests/paper-claim-inventory-v1.json; the builder reconstructs the index without pooling study populations.

## RQ1: workflow defects and practical consequences

The original diagnostic collection retained 75 initial units. After measurement audit, it contains one exposed native-only check mismatch, eight measurement-invalid Quartz comparisons, 12 unresolved outcomes, 26 rejected prefixes, 26 neither-mismatch outcomes and two singletons. The one native-only mismatch is the known PetClinic name case, not a newly discovered independently adjudicated defect. The corrected Quartz coverage remains a separate cohort with five prefix rejections, 14 neither-mismatch and three unresolved cases.

The production extension has 150 initial boundary variants plus 64 initial controls, with follow-ups bringing the total to 358 pairs. Those are six purposively selected applications with correlated cases, not 358 independent natural-defect opportunities. Its zero baseline-native-only signals reflect a candidate construction informed by native constraints and shared baseline coverage. This does not show that mixed-interface failures never happen.

The later independent-input and benchmark results likewise report no confirmed new natural defects. They strengthen the descriptive negative evidence under their selection and qualification rules; they do not erase the historical examples or turn exposed cases into independent validation.

## RQ2: dependency-guided selection under equal budgets

The original completion manifest explicitly lists timed selector efficacy as not established. The common executor's successful control pairs qualify engineering behavior, not discovery advantage. Neither the later production extension nor the independently specified input studies supplies the missing equal-budget guided-versus-random comparison with the mandatory dependency-coverage comparator. The manuscript's original method-effectiveness claim therefore remains unsupported.

## RQ3: effects of guidance and downstream checks

The manual-contract ablation is informative about baseline information: adding agreement checks yields zero additional alerting records beyond complete transition contracts in the 44 exposed historical records. This supports preserving a strong comparator, not a new detection advantage. The NetBox comparison represents one known fault; equally informed runtime-configuration and parameterized references agree. Automatic semantic inference was not evaluated.

RESTestBench qualification retains 228 registered mutants and 106 passing valid controls. Supplied golden tests produce 227 execution-qualified assertion failures; one mutant fails before its test. These are golden-test outcomes, not measurements of a new generator. The Vibe pilot's 130 qualified comparisons yield 50 aggregate kills and 50 identified new failures, with zero disagreements. Its labels were exposed, and semantic fault adjudication is incomplete. Neither study provides the original full guidance-by-oracle experimental design.

The converter pilot supplies 192 preserved paired observations across two qualified adapters; a third adapter has 96 unexecuted pairs because operation binding failed. Synthetic repeated cases and adapter qualification cannot be counted as independent natural application defects.

## RQ4: agent exposure and task completion

No agent-performance intervention is established by these studies. Executing released model-generated test suites is not equivalent to measuring agents completing application tasks. The prospective manuscript's possible 432-episode grid remains a proposal, not a result or a reason to spend the remaining budget.

## Publication decision

The full evidence inventory does not rescue the original selector-effectiveness contribution. The supported material is a set of bounded empirical and measurement findings with substantial selection and qualification limits. It is suitable for preserving as a reproducible research record; this review does not establish Q1 submission readiness or recommend presenting it as a validated new testing method.

No additional runtime study follows automatically from this inventory. A new method study would need a concrete capability beyond complete manual contracts and established test generation, while a new empirical study would need an independent population and a reason its findings would materially advance knowledge. The existing protocols and their stopping rules remain closed. No case counts are summed across the ten source manifests into a purported sample size.
