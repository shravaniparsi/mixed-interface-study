## Research decision after this frame

This frame has not established a sufficiently broad independent fault benchmark. The sole explicit candidate supplies useful historical mechanism evidence, but its constructor exceptions are all caught by an issue-informed API predicate. Do not expand the search or mine the fourteen adjacent reports simply until a favorable exception appears. Additional pages, new search terms or app reconstruction require a scientifically distinct, bounded design before collection, with a defined sampling unit and missing-case accounting.

A concrete method contribution remains unresolved. More runtime checks cannot by themselves fix that gap. Before any new experimental cohort, identify what the proposed method does that a competent information-matched baseline does not, and a measurable outcome other than the number of hand-selected failures. If the claimed value is discovering constraints rather than executing them, then the next design must compare constraint acquisition under equal source access, charge the cost of extracting and validating rules, and reserve independent evaluation inputs before development. The present issue-informed predicate experiment does not evaluate automatic constraint acquisition or prove it impossible.

The alternative empirical contribution also remains open: a well-defined independent sample could characterize where API/native workflows diverge, including null findings. The current heterogeneous, exposed and convenience-selected cohorts cannot support a broad prevalence claim through pooling. These are two possible research directions, not completed contributions. No new cohort or performance claim is registered by this note.
