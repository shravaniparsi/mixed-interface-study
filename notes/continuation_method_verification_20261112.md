# Independent method-scope verification

The claim-source-verification skill required a fresh-context skeptic. The reviewer independently checked the proposed source/claim pairings against full method sections. Three pairings were assessed: two narrowed method descriptions confirmed; one affirmative novelty implication rejected as unsupported. This is not an exhaustive literature review.

SABench, sections 2 and 4 plus Appendix A.2, defines goal predicates over replayed state and authorization checks on successful writes. The replay includes unauthorized writes; authorization is a separate verdict. Supporting quotation: “The replay is pure-functional: it constructs the post-state used for scoring without changing the original environment.” This described method does not establish a later native-browser edit after API creation.

AgentRewardBench section 3.1 defines trajectory annotations for success, side effects and repetition. Supporting quotation: “Agreement with respect to success is the primary criterion with which we evaluate LLM judges.” Experts can inspect environments when screenshots are insufficient; do not describe their evaluation as exclusively screenshot-based. Their annotation procedure does not establish a controlled pairing of creation routes followed by matching native edits.
https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08942v2#S3.SS1

Rejected implication: these two sources establish novelty of our continuation test. They establish their own methods and adjacent scope, not the absence of other GUI/API work. Older validation-inconsistency and active semantic-testing work remains relevant. No claim of priority or detector superiority follows.
