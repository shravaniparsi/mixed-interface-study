# Selected-cohort contribution assessment

## Decision

The registered benchmark extension is closed under its stopping rules. It does not establish a Q1-ready research contribution. It adds reproducible observations and exposes limits of the three-path comparison, but it supplies neither a qualifying incremental defect signal nor sufficiently broad valid comparisons to establish equivalence or prevalence. Do not submit a paper claiming that the proposed detector finds defects missed by an information-matched API baseline on the strength of this cohort.

This is an assessment of the evidence, not a prediction of journal acceptance. A negative result can be publishable, but this particular null is weakened by purposive selection, one application reaching scientific execution, substantial unexecuted or unqualified comparisons, and unresolved novelty. More repetitions of these same cases would not address those problems.

## Selection and attrition

The pinned external frame contained 812 tasks. Full intent/template exposure preceded analyst coding of six continuation families containing 42 task instances. Deterministic representative selection reduced discretion within those families, but did not make family selection random, representative, or untouched. The task counts are not counts of independent applications or defects. Selected evaluator definitions were not used as an outcome oracle.

Four GitLab families received six frozen input specifications each, for 24 specifications. Issue assignment reached all scheduled entries. README reached all six specifications in the second repetition, while its first repetition stopped before scientific production. Membership and merge-request reviewer families each retained six unexecuted specifications after closure. Reddit and directory/file families closed during contract eligibility and contribute no enumerated scientific inputs; they still belong in the six-family selection denominator.

## What the measurements support

The two dispatched scientific workflows scheduled 24 triples and 72 branch entries. Eighteen triples have all three records, but a record does not imply valid production or a qualified comparison. There are 54 scientific records, 51 entries into the production phase, seven observed ordinary controls out of eight scheduled, and 18 unreached scientific entries. Six all-paths-preserve triples represent only three issue specifications repeated twice. There are zero candidate incremental signals and zero confirmed natural defects under the frozen rules.

The issue confidentiality change remains an unresolved observation: API/native changed the field, but native production did not establish a matching comparator. It cannot be credited as an incremental defect. The README branch-list failures occurred in native/native and API/API paths, with later snapshots containing the expected branch. Their cause is unresolved; successful API/native behavior does not support the proposed failure mechanism. Existing README creation was rejected by both API and native interfaces with unchanged repository snapshots, so that lifecycle case supports the competent baseline rather than a new defect claim.

The earlier independent-input cohort and converter pilot remain separate studies with their original selection limitations and denominators. Their repeated pairs cannot be pooled with these branch records to manufacture independent sample size. Known-fault variants and synthetic mutants cannot be presented as newly discovered natural defects. See notes/independent_sampling_decision_20261112.md and the closed-study manifests for those scopes.

The two scientific runs consumed 3,940 job-seconds (65.67 minutes). The wider archive-scoped benchmark total includes setup, inspection and failed qualification: 16,089 seconds (268.15 minutes). Neither elapsed-minute number is a dollar invoice. No further paid service or experiment is initiated by this assessment.

## What would materially change the research position

A further study would need a substantive design change, not replacement cases selected for likely failure. The immediate scientific problem is measurement validity: a stable, prospectively specified readback endpoint must distinguish durable state from transient visibility without silently turning failures into passes. That procedure would need qualification independent of the evaluation cases before a new freeze.

The contribution also needs a population and claim that this budget can actually test: an independently defined sampling frame spanning multiple eligible applications, information-matched baselines, and retained eligibility losses. A mechanism claim would need causal evidence beyond a selector failure or unmatched producer states. A method claim would need a demonstrated benefit beyond existing GUI/API semantic testing and hybrid-agent evaluation. The existing novelty screen is not an exhaustive proof of novelty.

Those are prerequisites for proposing another study, not authorization to reopen this cohort or an assurance that another study will yield positive results. A benchmark-eligibility or measurement-validity paper is a possible different research question, but the current exclusions alone do not validate it. The present material is an honest feasibility and empirical-study artifact, not a completed Q1 submission.
