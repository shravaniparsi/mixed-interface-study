This implementation does not certify every accepted/error path. It does not add database captures or make timing comparable to the database-instrumented v7 runner.

## Bounded exposed campaign and verification

The v8 supervisor executes the nine registered initial jobs once in their fixed order: two paired reference controls, five paired candidates, then two native singletons. Either reference must be clean before proceeding. The registered terminal action controls continuation; a halt on the final job still produces a halted campaign. Suggested repeats are retained as decisions but never dispatched in this engineering sweep. There is no resume, replacement, retry, primary promotion or inference of an independent sample count.

Each job has a 240-second worker deadline. Cleanup allows five seconds for termination and two for killing, plus the helper's final wait. Nine job budgets, cleanup and 120 seconds of controller allowance fit within the 2400-second outer deadline. The hosted execution step allows 42 minutes and the overall workflow 55 minutes, including separately bounded setup. These are ceilings, not expected runtime or measured scientific costs.

The controller checkpoints before launch and after source-qualified extraction. Every attempt gets a new directory and process group. Executor errors are retained and halt execution. The independent verifier reconstructs each observation from raw evidence using the qualified loader, checks source/selection/bootstrap/commit bindings, validates the exact attempt prefix, and replays stop decisions. Extra attempts, forged completion and dispatch after a halt fail verification. Executor/setup failures retain artifacts but do not receive a semantic sweep certificate.

Eight orchestration tests cover complete and halted sweeps, unknown policies, last-singleton halts, changed identities, singleton promotion, executor exceptions, forged accounting and timeout arithmetic. The hosted workflow is manual and restricted by the exact exposed selection. Its results remain engineering evidence, pending review of the retained run.
