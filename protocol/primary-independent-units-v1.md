# Prospective independent diagnostic units v1

The primary diagnostic collection retains the entire registered corpus: 73 paired entries and two native-only controls. Every entry is a separately declared diagnostic unit with a fresh hosted environment. Its initial unit executes the exact candidate once, followed, for a non-reference pair, by its source-identical reference case under the candidate's runtime instrumentation. Duplicate reference executions remain control repetitions, not additional catalog entries or independent samples.

This is a prospective batching amendment to the earlier single-process whole-corpus sweep. A halt stops its current unit, preserving unstarted work. Other predeclared independent units use fresh hosted environments and remain scheduled. No result from a failed unit may be replaced, resumed or silently substituted. This scope prevents one unreadable application state from censoring unrelated independently reset candidates. It applies to all cases before untouched Quartz execution, not selectively to observed failures.

After every initial unit has a retained status, the immutable ledger determines follow-ups. A terminal-v2 repeat-eligible candidate with a clean same-instrumentation initial reference receives repetition two, candidate then reference. Repetition three is permitted only after repetition two finishes without a halt and its reference is clean. A candidate acting as its own reference must satisfy the same clean-reference rule; a failed positive control is a blocked unit, not an adjudicated defect. Each repetition has a unique fixed attempt identifier. Incomplete units, failed references and unavailable branches remain explicit. No ad hoc reruns are allowed.

The common requirement-based interpretation and all registered payloads remain unchanged. Eight RealWorld rejection-oriented cases have a known unsupported accepted-state continuation path. If the pinned application unexpectedly accepts such a representation, retain its raw response and fail-closed missing/unsupported evidence; do not normalize it into valid input, invent a continuation, credit coverage or classify interface superiority. This declares a measurement limitation, not evidence that acceptance is impossible. Such cases remain in attempted/blocked denominators. Existing unreadable-state and unknown-policy outcomes likewise remain unresolved.

Native-only controls are never converted to pairs. Initial, control and repeat observations stay separate. The collection establishes descriptive response and continuation observations; it does not itself adjudicate distinct defects or constitute the later timed selector comparison.

Each unit has a 900-second application envelope and a 30-minute hosted-job ceiling including dependencies and artifact retention. Native pair limits remain bounded by the existing runner profiles. At most 75 initial and 146 follow-up hosted units are planned before halts/control checks reduce the follow-up count. Effective source and environment acceptance must be frozen and verified before dispatch. This document alone does not authorize primary execution.
