# RealWorld username identity contract preparation

Status: unit-tested preparation only. No new application execution, dispatch qualification, coverage promotion, primary freeze, or independent sample count.

The existing qualified adapter and extractors remain version-pinned. Their profile normalization excludes identity changes, and their full-state snapshot queries the fixture username literally. A renamed account therefore requires a separate adapter and extractor contract before execution can qualify a continuation.

`scripts/realworld_identity_v1.mjs` prepares two components. The accepted-state expectation preserves a nonempty submitted username exactly and applies the existing null/empty bio and image representation rules. This is a candidate comparison rule, not evidence of server behavior or a decision that a username must be accepted. Email changes and credentials remain outside scope. Unexpected acceptance of null/empty usernames remains unsupported.

The identity snapshot requires an explicit expected username and email plus the nonempty unique article references established by the restored fixture. It queries the URL-encoded expected username, probes every pinned article reference even if the author query returns zero, and checks current principal, exact article membership, article author identities, response completeness, unique comment identities and exact probe inventory. Raw successful responses and failed-contract responses remain in the reads array; transport exceptions retain prior responses. Authentication tokens are excluded from the projected user state. This contract concerns the current account and its pinned articles, global tags, and comments on those articles. It does not establish immutable database user identity or inspect unrelated accounts.

A binding or structure failure produces an unreadable snapshot, not unchanged state, a semantic defect, or a stable mismatch. The future extractor must retain the specific failure and apply the common terminal policy. Nested article state remains raw except for tag membership ordering, as in the previous snapshot contract. Fixture membership checking is specific to username updates, which do not create or delete articles; this helper must not be reused unchanged for article creation workflows.

The sixteen tests use synthetic responses. They cover renamed principals, URL encoding, length preservation without length-policy inference, optional fields, unsupported identity edits, wrong users, stale author queries, missing/foreign/duplicate articles, pagination, stale nested authors, malformed comments, duplicate/unreadable/unregistered probes, response ordering, transport errors and pre-request fixture guards. These are engineering checks and are not research observations.

Next integration must (1) introduce a versioned live adapter without modifying archived source contracts, (2) bind fixture references and expected post-prefix principal before continuation, (3) verify fresh UI authentication against the expected identity rather than the old username, (4) independently reconstruct the same checks in the extractor from captured requests and responses, (5) preserve the registered unknown policy for the 60/61-character username cases and the rejection policy for null/empty cases, and (6) qualify a fresh bounded campaign only after unit and replay checks pass. Existing corpus gaps remain open until those steps complete.
